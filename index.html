End-to-End Pipeline

How Receipt Guru AI Works

A multi-stage vision and reasoning pipeline designed for accuracy and fraud deterrence.

01
📸

Upload Snapshot

User captures receipt via smartphone camera or drops an invoice image/PDF.

02
🔍

AI & OCR Extract

Gemini Vision and Tesseract OCR parse raw pixel text into Merchant, Date, Tax, and Amount.

03
🗂️

Auto-Classify

Expenses are dynamically sorted into categories like Food, Travel, Supplies, or Tech.

04
🚨

Analyze & Audit

Heuristics cross-reference earlier entries to detect identical claims and unusual spikes.

05
📊

Visualize & Export

Instant visual financial dashboard with Chart.js charts and exportable ledger data.
